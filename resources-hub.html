The Heart of Lanternwood

Lanternwood is not about finding the perfect path. It is about continuing together when no path is obvious.

These resources are designed to support the same idea: children do not need every answer before they take the next step. Sometimes they simply need a steady hand, a safe place, and someone willing to walk with them.
